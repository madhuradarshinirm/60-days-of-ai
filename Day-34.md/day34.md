# Day 34 – Marketing Detective

## Objective

Built and tested an AI-powered Marketing Detective simulator using Claude AI.

## Features

- Interactive Marketing Investigation
- Campaign Evidence Board
- Drag-and-Drop Evidence Organization
- Marketing Mistake Identification
- Expert Verdict and Explanation
- Marketing Learning Report
- Replay with Randomized Cases
- Responsive HTML Application

## Activities Performed

- Generated the complete HTML application.
- Opened the simulator in the browser.
- Selected the preferred theme.
- Accepted the first marketing investigation.
- Analyzed campaign evidence using the investigation board.
- Organized evidence cards.
- Identified the primary marketing mistake.
- Reviewed the expert explanation and final verdict.
- Studied the Marketing Learning Report.
- Replayed the simulator using another randomized case.

## Key Learnings

- Marketing investigations require analyzing multiple performance indicators.
- Audience targeting greatly influences campaign success.
- Evidence-based decision making improves marketing strategies.
- AI can simplify campaign diagnostics and learning.
- Interactive simulations make marketing concepts easier to understand.
