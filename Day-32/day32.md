# Day 32 – Marketing Strategy Simulator

## Objective

Built and tested an AI-powered Marketing Strategy Simulator using Claude AI.

## Features

- Business / Personal Brand / Random Client modes
- Target Audience Analysis
- Marketing Platform Selection
- Content Pillar Planning
- AI-generated 30-Day Marketing Roadmap
- Random Marketing Event Simulation
- Growth Performance Report
- Reusable Claude Marketing Prompts
- Responsive Interactive HTML Application

## Activities Performed

- Generated the complete HTML application.
- Opened the simulator in the browser.
- Selected Business mode.
- Defined the target audience.
- Selected the most suitable marketing platforms.
- Chose three content pillars.
- Generated the 30-day marketing roadmap.
- Responded to a randomized marketing event.
- Reviewed the final growth report.
- Explored reusable Claude prompts.
- Replayed the simulation using another scenario.

## Key Learnings

- AI can accelerate marketing strategy creation.
- Choosing the right audience improves campaign performance.
- Content pillars help maintain consistency.
- Different platforms require different marketing approaches.
- AI-generated roadmaps provide structured planning.
