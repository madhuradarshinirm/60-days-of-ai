How the Control Tower works

A quick briefing before you take the seat.

Objective

Keep the network healthy for 3 minutes. Alerts arrive continuously and speed up over time. Each one needs a decision before its timer runs out.

KPIs

  • Service Level / Customer Satisfaction — how well you're meeting commitments.
  • Inventory Health / Transportation Efficiency — the physical network's condition.
  • Operating Cost — spend relative to budget; lower is better.
  • Revenue Protected — dollars saved by good decisions.

Actions

Each alert offers several responses. The best-fit action improves KPIs and raises your score; mismatched or ignored actions cost you — sometimes immediately, sometimes a few seconds later, once the consequence "lands."

Controls

Use ⏸ to pause, 🔊 to toggle the sound indicator, and the timer to track your shift. Good luck, Chief.
