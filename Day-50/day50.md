# Day 50 – Defend Your Experience

## Overview

Today I explored the Defend Your Experience application using Claude.

The application helped me practice explaining and defending the experiences, projects, and skills included in my resume or portfolio.

## What I Did

- Generated the Defend Your Experience application using Claude
- Uploaded my resume/portfolio
- Practiced defending my project and experience claims
- Answered interview-style questions
- Reviewed the AI-generated Defense Report

## Interview Preparation

The activity helped me understand the importance of explaining:

- My actual contribution to a project
- Technologies used
- Problems encountered
- Solutions implemented
- Results achieved
- Key lessons learned

## Key Learning

I learned that having a project on a resume is not enough. I should also be able to clearly explain what I personally contributed, why I selected a particular technology, what challenges I faced, and how I solved them.

## Key Takeaways

- Resume claims should be supported by real experience.
- Technical interviews may ask detailed follow-up questions.
- Clear explanations are important when defending projects.
- Understanding my own projects deeply improves interview confidence.
- AI can be useful for generating realistic interview practice.
