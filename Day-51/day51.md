# Day 51 – Capstone: Product Discovery & Sprint Planning

## Capstone Project

### AI Career & Interview Copilot

An AI-powered career preparation platform designed to help college students and fresh graduates prepare for internships and entry-level software engineering roles.

## Problem

Students often struggle to understand whether their resume matches their target roles, which skills they are missing, and how to organize their interview preparation.

Career preparation resources are often scattered across different platforms, making the process difficult to manage.

## Target Users

- College students
- Final-year CSE/IT students
- Fresh graduates
- Students preparing for internships
- Students preparing for entry-level software engineering roles

## Proposed Solution

The AI Career & Interview Copilot provides:

- Resume analysis
- Skill-gap identification
- Personalized preparation roadmap
- Technical interview practice
- HR interview practice
- AI-generated feedback
- Progress tracking

## v1.0 Scope

### Included

1. Resume analysis
2. Skill-gap analysis
3. Personalized preparation plan
4. Technical interview simulator
5. HR interview simulator
6. AI feedback
7. Progress tracking

### Excluded from v1.0

- Automatic job applications
- Recruiter integrations
- Employer dashboard
- Payment system
- Mobile application
- Complex authentication

## Key Learning

Today I learned how product discovery should happen before implementation.

Instead of immediately starting development, I defined the target users, problem, solution, MVP scope, exclusions, and success criteria.
