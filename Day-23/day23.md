# Day 23 – Customer & MVP Blueprint

## Objective
Built a complete Customer & MVP Blueprint for the CAFE24 startup using Claude AI.

## Deliverables
- Executive Summary
- Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
- Buyer Persona
- Top 10 Customer Pain Points
- Customer Journey Map
- Buying Triggers & Objections

- MoSCoW Prioritization

- Top 5 Startup Risks

- Overall Recommendation

- Understood customer pain points and buying behavior.
- Designed an MVP roadmap using MoSCoW prioritization.
- Explored pricing strategies and startup risk assessment.
- Created a structured action plan for launching the startup.
