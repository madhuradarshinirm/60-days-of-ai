## Task
Built and tested the Task Compass interactive organizational thinking application using Claude.

## Objective
The Task Compass activity helped me understand how workplace tasks can be assigned, routed, and coordinated between different people and teams.

- Completed Stage 1: Who Owns This?

## Key Learnings

1. Clear ownership is important for completing tasks efficiently.
2. Tasks should be routed according to responsibility and expertise.
3. Collaboration helps avoid duplicated work and communication gaps.
4. Organizational problems can often be improved by clearly defining roles and workflows.
5. Interactive simulations can make workplace decision-making easier to understand.
