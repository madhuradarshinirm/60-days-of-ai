# Day 29 – Operation Lifeline: AI Supply Chain Crisis Simulator

## Overview

Today I built and tested the **Operation Lifeline** simulator using Claude AI. The simulator demonstrates how AI can be used to manage supply chain disruptions, make executive decisions, negotiate with suppliers, and improve business resilience through interactive simulations.

---

## Objective

- Generate an interactive HTML Supply Chain Crisis Simulator.
- Simulate real-world supply chain disruptions.
- Practice executive decision-making during crisis situations.
- Analyze business performance using AI-generated dashboards.

---

## Tasks Completed

- Generated the complete HTML application using Claude.
- Opened the simulator in the browser.
- Reviewed the generated company profile.
- Analyzed the assigned supply chain crisis.
- Selected three crisis response actions.
- Completed supplier negotiation rounds.
- Answered CEO leadership questions.
- Selected AI-powered supply chain investments.
- Reviewed the executive dashboard and business scores.
- Tested the simulator with different crisis scenarios.
- Saved the HTML file.
- Captured screenshots of important sections.
